# Where fixture data comes from

Wattage is the fixture's power draw. LED fixtures use the maker's maximum or nominal draw. Tungsten and HMI fixtures use the lamp's wattage. Rows added on 2026-11-17 were collected by web search. The sources for each group are listed below.

## From the maker's own pages or datasheets
- **Astera:** astera-led.com product pages, using "LED power draw" where shown. Pages checked: AX2, AX3, AX5, AX7, AX9, AX10, NYX, HydraPanel, PixelBrick, QuikSpot, QuikPunch, QuikBeam, Pluto, Leo. SolaBulb and LunaBulb come from Astera's event catalog. LunaBulb (3.3 W) is rounded to 3.
- **ARRI:** the SkyPanel X tech data (X21/X22/X23), the L5 datasheet (L5-C, 115 W max) and SkyPanel datasheets.
- **Litepanels:** the Litepanels / Anton Bauer run-time chart (2016). It covers Astra, Inca, Sola, Hilio, Caliber, Lykos and Brick.
- **Aputure:** the help centre and product pages for LS 1200d Pro, LS 600c Pro, STORM 400x and STORM 1000c (1300 W is Aputure's figure; retailers list 1200 W to 1440 W). amaran 100d S, 200d S, 150c, 300c and the F21/F22 flexible mats use the maximum input from the Aputure help centre.
- **Creamsource:** the knowledge base (Vortex24, 1950 W) and the Vortex4 datasheet (325 W).
- **Mole-Richardson:** mole.com (Baby LED 150 W) and the SeniorLED spec sheet (900 W).
- **ETC:** the Desire D60 datasheet (161 W), the fos/4 Fresnel 7in datasheet (178 W typical, Lustr X8) and the Source Four LED Series 3 datasheet (307 W, Lustr X8).

## From rental-house or retailer listings
- Kino Flo FreeStyle 21, FreeStyle T44 and Celeb 850 come from Panavision rental listings. Celeb 250 is worked out from Adorama's 1.2 A at 120 V.
- Quasar Rainbow 2 and Double Rainbow come from Full Compass and Adorama.
- Mole Junior LED (200 W) and Tener LED (1600 W) come from Adorama.
- The SkyPanel S120-C figure (400 W) comes from retailers.
- Aputure LS 600x Pro (720 W) comes from a third-party comparison table.
- Aputure STORM 80c (100 W) comes from Adorama and Vistek. Electro Storm XT26 (3500 W AC maximum) comes from retailers; Aputure's shop gives 3200 W DC.
- Kino Flo MIMIK 120 (400 W maximum, 200 W average) comes from CVP and B&H.

## Not confirmed by any source (best known value, check before trusting)
- ARRI L7-C (220 W) and L10-C (510 W).
- Aputure Nova P300c (360 W) and B7c (7 W).
- Kino Flo 2ft 4Bank (120 W).
- Lowel Ego (55 W).
- LiteGear LiteMat Plus 8 and Spectrum 8 (400 W). This assumes double the 4.
- Legacy tungsten and HMI model names and lamp sizes from memory:
  - Mole-Richardson open faces, Brutes, Skypan and Molebeam;
  - LTM Pepper and Cinepar;
  - Desisti Leonardo 10K and Rembrandt;
  - ARRI Daylight Compact, ARRISUN, Pocket PAR and True Blue D series;
  - Joker-Bug 200.
- Power-unit column:
  - Aputure STORM 400x and 1000c are marked as having no separate driver;
  - Creamsource Vortex4 is marked as using an external driver.
